?

Navigate through a series of caves while trying to find and shoot the wumpus, while avoiding hazards like pits or bats.

How to Play:

*Note: For the following parts, adjacent means up, down, left, or right, not diagonals.

Movement:

Click the move buttons to move to rooms adjacent to your player. Your player's room is marked in white on the grid. It is also shown on the HUD. Rooms that your player have been to turn gray. Each time you move, your turns go up by one.

Alerts and Hazards:

Hazards spawn randomly upon game start, in different rooms. Being in a room adjacent to a hazard triggers its alert. Being in the room of the hazard triggers its action.

Bats:

There are 2 bats. Alert: "You hear high pitched squeaking!". Action: The bats transport you to a completely random room. It is possible the room it transports you to may contain a hazard, in which that case you immediately lose.

Pits:

There are 2 pits. Alert: "You feel a draft!". Action: You immediately lose.

Wumpus:

There is 1 wumpus. Alert: "You smell a horrid stench!". Action: You immediately lose.

Shooting and GameOver

You start with 3 bullets, and could shoot into adjacent rooms by using the shoot buttons. Upon clicking on a shoot button, your bullets decrease. If you run out of bullets, you lose. If you successfully shoot the wumpus, you win. If you're in a room adjacent to a wumpus, but miss, the wumpus moves randomly to an adjacent room. It is possible through this way for the wumpus to enter a room with a hazard, a room the player visited before, or the room the player is in (in which case you lose).
